Refund Policy.

What happens to your money if a shoot is cancelled, moved or does not go as planned. Your written quote may set different terms for a specific project; where it does, the quote applies.

In short

The downpayment holds your date. Cancel with enough notice and we refund it less any costs we have already committed. Cancel late and the downpayment is kept. If we cancel, you get everything back. Finished work is not refundable, but we fix anything that does not match the brief.

What this policy covers

This policy applies to downpayments, project fees and other amounts paid to Lunarcore Studios ("Lunarcore", "we", "us") for event coverage, video, photography, live streams, social media and design work. It works together with our Terms & Conditions. If your signed quote or agreement sets a different refund rule for a project, that rule applies.

The downpayment

A booking is confirmed once you accept the quote in writing and we receive the downpayment stated in it. From that moment we turn down other work for your date and commit crew, equipment and, where needed, travel and venue arrangements. That is why the downpayment is refundable only as set out below.

If you cancel

  • 14 days or more before the shoot: we refund the downpayment, less any third-party costs we have already paid on your behalf and cannot recover (for example talent, rentals, permits or travel). We will show you these costs.
  • Less than 14 days before the shoot: the downpayment is kept. If you have paid more than the downpayment, the difference is refunded less unrecoverable costs.
  • Less than 48 hours before, or on the day: the full project fee is due, because the crew and equipment have been booked and cannot be reassigned.

Instead of cancelling, you can usually move the date. See Rescheduling.

Rescheduling

  • One reschedule with at least 7 days' notice is free. Your downpayment moves to the new date, which must be within 90 days of the original date and subject to our availability.
  • A second reschedule, or one with less than 7 days' notice, may carry a rebooking fee stated in your quote, plus any unrecoverable third-party costs.
  • If no new date can be agreed within 90 days, the booking is treated as cancelled under If you cancel, counted from the date you first asked to move it.

If we cancel

If Lunarcore has to cancel a confirmed shoot for a reason within our control, we refund everything you have paid in full, within 7 business days. Where we can, we will first offer a replacement crew or a new date at no extra cost.

Weather, disasters and other events outside anyone's control

If a shoot cannot go ahead because of severe weather, natural disasters, government restrictions, venue closure or similar events outside both parties' control, we reschedule rather than cancel, with no rebooking fee. If a new date cannot be agreed within 90 days, we refund the downpayment less unrecoverable third-party costs.

Finished and delivered work

Fees for work that has been delivered are not refundable, because the time, crew and production have already been spent. If something in the delivery does not match the agreed brief or quote, tell us within 7 days of delivery and we will correct it within the revision rounds in your quote. Changes of mind, new creative directions or uses not covered by the brief are quoted as additional work.

Partially completed work

If a project stops partway through at your request, you pay for the work completed up to that point, pro-rated against the quote, plus unrecoverable third-party costs. Any balance already paid above that amount is refunded.

How to ask for a refund

Email lunarcorestudios@gmail.com with your booking reference (it starts with LC-), the shoot date and the reason. We confirm receipt within 2 business days and reply with a decision within 7 business days. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method or bank account within 10 business days; your bank or e-wallet may take a few more days to show it.

Your rights

Nothing in this policy removes the rights you have under the Philippine Consumer Act (Republic Act No. 7394) and other laws that cannot be waived. If we cannot resolve a concern with you directly, you may bring it to the Department of Trade and Industry.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on the date you book applies to your booking.
