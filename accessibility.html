Accessibility

Creativity for everyone.

We believe creativity should be accessible to everyone. We are committed to ensuring our website and content are inclusive, easy to navigate, and accessible to all users, including those with disabilities.

Last updated 22 October 2026

In short

We aim for WCAG 2.1 Level AA across the site. If anything gets in your way, or you need our content in another format, email or call us and we will sort it out promptly.

Our commitment

We strive to adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards wherever possible. This includes:

  • Providing clear, consistent navigation
  • Using readable fonts and sufficient colour contrast
  • Making our site navigable via keyboard
  • Adding alt text to images and visual media
  • Designing content that works well with screen readers
  • Respecting your "reduce motion" setting, so animations stay still when you ask them to

Continuous improvement

We are continuously working to identify and address accessibility barriers. As we grow, we aim to improve both our website and the experiences of our clients and visitors.

If you experience any difficulty accessing content, or if you have suggestions for improvement, we would love to hear from you.

Partial compliance due to third-party content

While we aim to ensure accessibility across our entire website, some third-party content (like embedded videos, forms, or social media widgets) may not be fully accessible. We recommend checking the accessibility features of those platforms for additional support.
