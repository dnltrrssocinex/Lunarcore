Enquiries and bookings

  • Sending a brief or picking a time on the calendar is a request. It does not reserve the date.
  • We reply with a written quote covering scope, crew, deliverables, timeline and price.
  • Your booking is confirmed once you accept the quote in writing and we receive the downpayment stated in it.
  • Prices on a quote are valid for the period stated on it.

Payment

Payment terms are set out in your quote. Unless the quote says otherwise, a downpayment secures your date and the balance is due before the final files are delivered. Late payment can delay delivery.

Rescheduling and cancellation

Tell us as early as possible if you need to move or cancel. We will try to move your shoot to another available date. Because we turn down other work and commit crew and equipment once a date is confirmed, the downpayment may be non-refundable after confirmation; your quote states the exact cancellation terms.

If a shoot cannot go ahead because of events outside anyone's control, such as severe weather, natural disasters or government restrictions, we will work with you to reschedule rather than cancel.

What we need from you

  • An accurate brief, and access to the venue at the agreed call time
  • Any permits or venue permissions needed to photograph, film or fly drones on location
  • Permission from talent, staff or guests to appear in the content, where needed
  • One contact person for approvals, and feedback within the agreed review window

Deliverables and revisions

Deliverables, turnaround times and the number of revision rounds are stated in your quote. Requests outside that scope, such as new edits, extra formats or added shoot time, are quoted separately. We keep delivered files for at least 90 days after delivery; please keep your own copies.

Ownership and use of the work

  • Once the project is paid in full, you may use the final deliverables for the purposes agreed in your quote.
  • Raw footage, unedited photos and project files stay with Lunarcore unless the quote says otherwise.
  • Licensed music, fonts and stock are covered only for the uses stated in the quote.
  • We may show the finished work in our portfolio and on social media. If the project is confidential, tell us in writing before the shoot and we will keep it private.
