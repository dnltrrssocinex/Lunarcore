Privacy Policy.

What we collect when you contact or book us, why we need it, and the choices you have. Written to follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

In short

We only ask for what we need to reply to you and run your shoot. We do not sell your details, we do not send newsletters you did not ask for, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

Lunarcore Studios ("Lunarcore", "we", "us") is an event, video and photo production studio based in the Philippines. We are the personal information controller for the details you share through this website.

Questions about this policy or your data go to our data protection contact at lunarcorestudios@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use Start a project or Book your event, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your business, brand or event name, and the role that best describes you
  • The services you are interested in, your timing, and the notes you write about the project
  • The date and time slot you pick on the booking calendar, and the type and length of the shoot

Our website host keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. Your browser stores one small setting that remembers whether the collections sidebar is open; it never leaves your device.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and match you with the right producer
  • To prepare a quote, hold and confirm your date, and send booking emails and calendar invites
  • To plan and deliver your project: crew call sheets, shot lists and file delivery
  • To keep the business and tax records the law requires

We will only send you marketing messages if you clearly agree to them, and you can opt out at any time.

Why we are allowed to

We process your information because you give your consent when you send a form, and because it is needed to take the steps you asked for before entering into a contract with us, and later to carry out that contract. Some records are kept because Philippine law requires it.

Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • The service providers that run our enquiry and booking system, email and calendar (for example our CRM, HighLevel / LeadConnector). They process it on our instructions only.
  • The crew members and partners assigned to your project, limited to what they need for the job
  • Government authorities, when the law requires it

Some of these providers store data on servers outside the Philippines. When that happens we rely on providers with appropriate safeguards and keep the same level of protection this policy describes.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not turn into a project are deleted within two years of our last conversation. Records for confirmed projects are kept for as long as we need them to deliver and support the work, and for the period Philippine tax and accounting rules require. After that we delete or anonymise them.

How we protect it

We use reasonable organisational, physical and technical measures to protect your information: access is limited to the people who need it, accounts are password protected, and the site is served over an encrypted connection. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach that affects you, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is processed
  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent you have given
  • Have your information erased or blocked
  • Receive a copy of your information in a common electronic format
  • Be compensated for damages caused by inaccurate, incomplete or unauthorised use of your information
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission

To use any of these rights, email lunarcorestudios@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will reply within 30 days.

People in our photos and videos

Our work often shows people at events and on set. We publish it with the permission of the client who hired us, and we ask clients to inform guests that the event is being photographed and filmed. If you appear in something on this website and would like it taken down, email us with a link and we will remove it.

Children

This website and our booking forms are meant for adults booking on behalf of themselves or a business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 through this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top always shows the latest version, and significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
